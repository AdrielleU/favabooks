# Month-end review

Review the books for the month the owner names (default: last month), following
AGENTS.md. Don't change anything; report.

1. Run the check; list any errors.
1. Transactions still flagged `!` (waiting in For review), and ones booked to
   `Expenses:Uncategorized`.
1. Expenses over $75 without a `document:` receipt.
1. Bank and card accounts with no transactions in the last two weeks of the
   month (a missed import?), and the last `balance` line of each.
1. Unpaid invoices and bills (`invoice:` entries still sitting in Accounts
   Receivable or Accounts Payable) and how old they are.
1. Anything unusual: duplicates, a payee booked to different categories, a
   subscription that changed price.

End with a short to-do list for the owner, most important first.
